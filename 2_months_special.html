Main future ka wada nahi karunga ke sab kuch hamesha perfect hoga, lekin itna zaroor kehunga ke tumhari izzat, tumhari khushi aur tumhari feelings mere liye important hain. ❤️

Happy 2 Months, Meri Jaan. 🥹❤️
